
Actual xkb config in the project is minimal: only the 'caps:shift_modifier' option from my other project.

This can be used together with one of the "compatibility options" named "both Shifts together ..." are Caps Lock or Shift lock etc. 
   
In Short: I recommend using the CapsLock and LSGT keys for Shift and AltGr and I do it in this order, but the reverse also works and is muscle-memory compatible with an ANSI keyboard.
